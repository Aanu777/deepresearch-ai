## Overview

DeepResearch AI is a full-stack AI-powered research platform designed to automate the traditionally manual research process.

Instead of simply generating an answer from a single model response, the platform is designed around a research workflow:

```text
Research Question
       │
       ▼
   AI Research
       │
       ├── Research Planning
       ├── Information Discovery
       ├── Source Collection
       ├── Evidence Analysis
       ├── Synthesis
       └── Report Generation
       │
       ▼
Structured Research Report
